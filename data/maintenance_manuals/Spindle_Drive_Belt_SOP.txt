Spindle Drive — Belt & RPM Troubleshooting SOP

Failure signature
Belt tension drift can produce intermittent vibration and RPM oscillation. The thermal signal may remain normal during early degradation.

Diagnostic steps
Check belt tension, pulley alignment, belt wear, and spindle RPM stability under load. Compare current RPM against the rated spindle speed.

Corrective action
If belt tension is outside specification or the belt shows cracking or glazing, replace DRIVE-BELT-HX and verify spindle alignment.

Historical pattern
Machine_04 maintenance records show intermittent vibration caused by belt tension drift. The issue was resolved after belt replacement and alignment.
